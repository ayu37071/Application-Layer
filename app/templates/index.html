QUIC (RFC 9000) operates on top of UDP in user-space to overcome fundamental TCP head-of-line bottlenecks:

  • 0-RTT Connection Establishment: Merges transport and TLS 1.3 cryptographic handshakes into a single round-trip.
  • No Head-of-Line (HoL) Blocking: Multiple HTTP streams are independently multiplexed. A dropped packet in Stream 1 never blocks Stream 2!
  • Connection Migration: Identifies connections via a 64-bit Connection ID rather than the IP:Port 4-tuple, enabling smooth transitions between Wi-Fi and 5G cellular.
